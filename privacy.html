Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026 • YouTube Multi-Uploader

This Privacy Policy describes how the personal automation application YouTube Multi-Uploader ("we", "our", or "the Application") collects, uses, and manages information when you connect Google services.

1. Nature of Application

YouTube Multi-Uploader is an automated internal utility built to schedule video publishing to the creator's personal YouTube channel from a designated Google Drive storage folder. It is not a commercial service or a public SaaS platform.

2. Data Accessed and Scopes Used

The Application requests the following permissions via Google OAuth 2.0:

  • YouTube Data API v3 (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube): Used strictly to upload videos and manage metadata (titles, descriptions, tags) on the user's authorized YouTube channel.
  • Google Drive API (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive): Used strictly to retrieve video files staged in the user's dedicated video storage folder and mark uploaded files.

3. Data Storage and Retention

We do not store your personal information, videos, or credentials on external third-party servers. All OAuth tokens and refresh tokens are stored securely in restricted environment variables or local credentials stores accessible only by the owner. No user data is sold, rented, or transferred to third parties.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

YouTube Multi-Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Revocation of Access

You can revoke the Application's access to your Google Account at any time by visiting your Google Account Permissions page at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
